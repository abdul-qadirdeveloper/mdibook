## Likely cause
**Hypothesis:** a caller of `POST /api/v1/appointments/{id}/reschedule` is sending the same slot again after getting `SLOT_TAKEN` (409). This is mostly on `doc_07` at lakeside, and it multiplies the conflicts. The spec says a lost slot is final (R23, R33: "the appointment is unchanged and R23 applies"), and CLAUDE.md says to never retry a conflict silently.

**Code I read:**
- In `src/lib/appointments/core.ts`, the function `rescheduleAppointment` turns a lost slot into `SLOT_TAKEN` in its catch block (lines 357–360), and that catch block does not retry.
- Unlike `bookAppointment` in the same file, `rescheduleAppointment` takes no idempotency key. So a repeated submission is not recognised as a repeat. It runs again and gets another conflict.
- `src/app/api/v1/appointments/[id]/reschedule/route.ts` (`POST`), `rescheduleAsPatient` in `src/lib/patient/reschedule.ts` and `rescheduleAsClinic` in `src/lib/clinic/reschedule.ts` only pass the call on. None of them retries.
- The patient appointments page (`src/app/patient/appointments/page.tsx`) says rescheduling is "Not available on this page yet". In the `.tsx` files under `src/app`, I found no screen that calls the reschedule endpoint.

So the retries most likely come from outside the server code in this repository: an external client, a script or an integration. I have not confirmed that.

**Alternative I have not ruled out:** real demand on `doc_07`'s open slots, for example many appointments being moved at once after a change to `doc_07`'s schedule. That would raise conflicts, but it would not easily explain the same-slot retry share going from 0.03 to 0.56. Whether anything changed on `doc_07`'s schedule: not known.

## Confidence, and what would change it
**Medium** that the cause is client-side same-slot retries; **low** on who the caller is.
- **Would raise it:** request logs showing one client or actor role sending the same appointment id and slot repeatedly after a 409. Or the rate dropping once that caller is stopped.
- **Would lower it:** a schedule change or time off for `doc_07` around 04:10–04:15 that triggered a wave of moves. Or the `sameSlotRetry` flag turning out to mean something else than "the same caller asked again for the same slot".
- **Not known:**
  - which internal doctor `doc_07` maps to
  - how `sameSlotRetry` is computed
  - whether anything was deployed or changed in configuration near 04:10
  - whether patients are affected beyond failed moves. R33 means a failed move leaves the appointment unchanged.
